Design exploration

Pages by level of detail, November 2026 Latest

Date of last change: 13 November 2026
User research status: Not for research. For team and stakeholder feedback

Most regulator questions sit at a level of zoom: national, area, site or individual movement. This exploration tests whether arranging the report pages by those levels makes the pages and filters easier to find and use. It starts from the round 2 draft.

Design rationale

  • The 4 user groups (senior regulator or policy advisor, data analyst, area or team manager, frontline or compliance officer) are a draft, not research findings. The labels on Home ("Usually used by...") are there to test whether people recognise themselves in them.
  • Analysts don't fit neatly into one level, so Explore data sits across all levels with every filter. Senior users and analysts share the same data but get different pages: a summary for one and exploration for the other.
  • The flags reuse the round 2 checks and thresholds, which are still placeholders. POPs and consignment code checks only count at sites with 10 or more relevant movements, so a site with 2 movements doesn't get flagged on a single record.
  • "Movements without carrier registration details" is a flag, although registration details aren't mandatory if the site gives a reason. This conflicts with the R-02 wording that it isn't a measure of compliance. The team needs to decide whether it belongs in the ranking.
  • Even with these rules, a high share of the invented sites have 3 or more flags. Real thresholds would need to be set with regulators so the list separates sites usefully.

Research questions

  • Do users understand the levels, and do they pick the right starting point for their task?
  • Would frontline officers use Find a site before a visit, and is the Site page enough on its own?
  • Is a permit comparison worth the cost of getting permit data? Which permit conditions matter most?
  • Do area managers trust a ranking by number of flags, or do some checks matter more than others?
  • Do senior users want anything else at national level, for example change compared with the previous period?

To check with a Power BI developer

  • Find a site: a single-select slicer with search, on a column that joins site name and permit number, plus a drill-through button that is only active when 1 site is selected. Check how slicer search matches text, and how it performs with thousands of sites.
  • Site page: drill-through with "Keep all filters" turned off, so only the site and the date range pass through.
  • Sync slicers per page: none on National summary, location only on Sites to review, all on the Explore data pages.
  • Flags and "Checks above threshold" as DAX measures, including a measure for the longest gap in days. Check performance with real volumes.
  • Breadcrumbs by level would be text and buttons. The region link would drill through to Sites to review with the region filter set.
  • A phone layout for the Site page, as officers may use it on site. Power BI phone layouts are separate and don't reflow.

Not possible with current data

  • Permit limits: DWT does not hold permit data. The comparison needs data from the Environment Agency, SEPA, Natural Resources Wales and NIEA.
  • Where waste came from: Receipt of Waste data has the carrier and receiving site, but not the producer or the site the waste left. "Who sends waste to whom" is limited to carrier and receiving site.
  • Late submissions: the prototype data has no submission date, so this can't be a flag yet.
  • A network diagram of carriers and sites would need a custom visual, which may be blocked in the Defra tenant. Tables with cross-filtering are used instead.

Not changed yet

  • All "Not changed yet" items from the round 2 draft still apply, including details only shown on hover (WCAG 1.4.13).
  • The Site page is long. A frontline version might stop after the most recent movements.
