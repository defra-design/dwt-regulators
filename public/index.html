DWT regulator reports

This page is the index for different versions of the Power BI report prototypes for regulators. They show layout, filters and drill-down, not the final Power BI build.

  • Report prototypes start page (the page the team has been using)

All the reports use data submitted to Digital Waste Tracking through the Receipt of Waste service (the API and spreadsheet upload) by receiving sites. They do not include permit records, the carrier, broker and dealer register, consignee returns, waste returns or producer and collection data.

Where a report refers to these, for example a carrier registration number or a consignment code, it shows what the receiving site submitted, not a check against another system.

! Warning Every site, carrier, permit and figure in these prototypes is invented sample data.
