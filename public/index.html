DWT Regulator Reports

Power BI report prototypes

Clickable mock-ups for co-design and user research. They show layout, filters and drill-down, not the final Power BI build.

Data source: Receipt of Waste submissions only

All the reports are built solely on data submitted to Digital Waste Tracking through the Receipt of Waste service (the API and spreadsheet upload) by receiving sites. They do not include data from other sources, such as permit records, the carrier, broker and dealer register, consignee returns, waste returns or producer and collection data. Where a report refers to these, for example a carrier registration number or a consignment code, it shows what the receiving site submitted, not a check against another system.

Illustrative only: every site, carrier, permit and figure in these prototypes is invented sample data.

Individual reports

R-01
Receiving Site Intake Dashboard

Waste received from DWT national down to a single receiving site and its individual waste movements.
R-02
Carrier Activity & Registration Report

Carriers by registration number, movements without carrier registration details, and drill-down to a carrier and its movements.
R-03
Hazardous Waste & POPs Intake Report

Hazardous waste and POPs received, with a view switch, data assurance checks and drill-down to a site and its movements.
R-04
Receiver Submission Activity Report

For policy leads and senior users: who is submitting data, periods of inactivity and changes in reporting, with drill-down to a receiver and its submissions.
